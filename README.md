# Bachelor-s-degree-dissertation
Driven by the intensification of global climate change and the ‘dual-carbon’ strategy, the oil refining industry, as an energy-intensive industry, is facing severe pressure to reduce emissions, and the traditional planning and scheduling method, which is centred on the operational efficiency of the enterprise, can hardly cope with the restrictions on carbon emissions imposed by the goal of ‘2030 Carbon Peak, 2060 Carbon Neutral’. Therefore, this study takes the actual production process of an oil refinery as the research object, establishes an optimisation model for the whole process of refining plan including carbon emission accounting, optimises the production plan scheduling by using reinforcement learning algorithms, and achieves the goal of carbon emission reduction under the premise of guaranteeing the profitability of production, and compares the performance of solving the multi-period large-scale production scheduling problem with that of the traditional algorithms, which shows that the method can obtain an optimised plan within a short period of time. The method can obtain a satisfactory solution in a short time, which provides a new way of thinking and practice for the production scheduling optimisation of oil refineries in the transformation of low-carbon economy.
